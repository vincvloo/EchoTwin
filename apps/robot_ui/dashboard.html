Three ways to capture a place

EchoTwin works from photos or a video, because every phone can do that. A Scaniverse scan or a LiDAR mesh is more accurate and already metric, but needs an app, or a device with a LiDAR sensor, which costs more. We tested all three.

Photos / video + VGGTScaniverse (phone app)LiDAR (iPad mesh)
NeedsAny phone cameraA phone with the appA device with a LiDAR sensor
ScaleTrue scale with a printed marker on the table; otherwise estimated from the phone height (stage height within about 5 % of the Scaniverse scan)MetricMetric (table top 0.75 m, ceiling 2.25 to 2.30 m)
Names the objectsYes: a detector, then a vision-model reviewNoNo
Plan in our testCleanest: armchairs, sofa, lampsWorks, noisier93 % of the floor, table legs visible
Processing hereAbout 2 to 3 min on a laptop GPUAbout 30 sAbout 40 s

Small things are the weak spot of photos: the reconstructed table is flat to only 2 to 3 cm, so objects under about 5 cm can vanish. Scaniverse and LiDAR data are not part of this repository (the LiDAR scan is licensed for non-commercial use).
