    Twin editor

    Real centimetres, table centre = 0, X to the right, Y away from you. A saved .zip holds twin.json plus images and meshes: edit it by hand, keep it, or load it on another laptop. 3D scans: export .glb or .obj from Polycam, Scaniverse or Luma.

    Use your phone

    Open this on a phone on the same Wi-Fi. Accept the certificate warning once. The phone can scan the table, listen and drive.

    Three ways to capture a place

    EchoTwin works from photos or a video, because every phone can do that. A Scaniverse scan or a LiDAR mesh is more accurate and already metric, but needs an app, or a device with a LiDAR sensor, which costs more. We tested all three.
