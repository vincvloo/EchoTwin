EchoTwin

Give it a room: a phone scan, a few photos or a video. It builds the map a sonar robot needs, finds the objects, and shows a simulated robot localizing itself with four ultrasonic sensors.

    Three ways to capture a place
    
    
    EchoTwin works from photos or a video, because every phone can do that. A Scaniverse scan or a LiDAR
      mesh is more accurate and already metric, but needs an app, or a device with a LiDAR sensor, which costs more.
      We tested all three on the same pipeline.

    Names the objectsYes: YOLO, then a vision-model reviewNoNo
    
        
    Map in our testCleanest: armchairs, sofa, lampsWorks, noisier93 % of the floor, table legs visible
